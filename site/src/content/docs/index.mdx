## Results, with their limits

An open connection is evidence. A silent port is not proof of closure.
GoMap distinguishes observed refusals, timeout-inferred filtering and other
unresolved failures, and reports how much of the requested scope it confirmed.

Read the [detection model](/gomap/docs/detection/) before interpreting a product
version, certificate name or port hint as a verified identity.
